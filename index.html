Opinion of value

Applying the four primary comps' average of $188.12/SF to the 5,647 SF heated area indicates roughly $1,062,000; the broader 9-comp average of $169.06/SF indicates roughly $955,000, and the comp set's median ($162.50/SF) indicates roughly $918,000. Weighing the primary, size- and use-comparable sales most heavily and tempering for the subject's specialized veterinary buildout (kennels, surgical suite, exam rooms) and 1949/1999 vintage, Grant-Murray Real Estate's opinion is an indicated value range of approximately $850,000 to $1,075,000, with a recommended list price of $975,000 (~$172.66/SF heated) to allow room for negotiation. This is a broker's opinion of value for marketing purposes, not an appraisal.
